Vanilla Cake

Welcome to the ultimate guide on how to bake a delicious vanilla cake. Follow this simple recipe and you'll be able to bring it to any occasion!

Ingredients

  • cooking spray
  • 2 1/3 cups of all purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups of white sugar
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup whole milk
  • 3/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9-inch cake pan with cooking spray
  2. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl. Add eggs, milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla. Mix until the batter is smooth. Pour into prepared pan.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven for 45 to 50 minutes. Insert a toothpick into the center of the cake to check that it comes out clean. Cool on a rack for 5 minutes. Run a table knife around the edges to loosen, and flip the pan over. Let the cake cool completely.
  4. Use a long serrated knife to slice the cake horizontally through the center. Spread frosting or filling between the layers and replace the top half of the cake. Frost the top and sides as desired.
  5. Enjoy!
